Project 03

Restoration & Repointing

Chimney Stack Rebuild
& Repointing, Droitwich

Sympathetic restoration of a deteriorated chimney stack on a Grade II listed farmhouse. Works included full stack rebuild above the flashing line using lime mortar, tuck-pointing to the original face brickwork, and new lead flashings — all approved by the local conservation officer.

Droitwich, WR9 Listed building Lime mortar Conservation 2025
